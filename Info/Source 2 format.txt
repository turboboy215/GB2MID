This is the later driver from Workboy and Double Dragon 3: The Arcade Game. It is not related to the earlier driver from Pyramids of Ra.

Song table
=======================================
In Workboy, the sound engine only uses the first 2 channels. In Double Dragon 3, channel 4 is also used. The song table is comprised of a series of 2-4 16-bit pointers per entry, as follows:

1-2. Channel 1 pointer
3-4. Channel 2 pointer
5-6. Channel 4 pointer (Double Dragon 3 only)

Song commands
=======================================
Songs use the following commands:
00-EF xx = Play note (00 = Rest) (X = Length in frames in Workboy/Length index from table in Double Dragon 3)
F0-FD = (No effect - all songs in Workboy start with F0)
FE = Restart song
FF = Stop music (all channels)
