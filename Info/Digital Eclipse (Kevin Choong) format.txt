Song table
=======================================
The song table consists of pointers to each channel of a song. 720° only uses channel 1 and 2 for music, with channel 3 reserved for sound effects using PCM samples. The other games additionally use channel 4 for music.

In 720°, the table is structured with entries as follows:
1-2. Channel 1 pointer
3-4. Channel 2 pointer
5. Bank
6-8. (Unused)

In all other games, the table is structured with entries as follows:
1-2. Channel 1 pointer
3-4. Channel 2 pointer
5-6. Channel 4 pointer
7. Bank
8-16. (Unused)

Song data
=======================================
The song data is a very simple format used for each channel. For channel 1 and 2, it is structured as follows:

1-2. Note frequency (FF FF = rest)
3. Note length

For channel 4, it is structured as follows:
1. NR43 value (FF = rest)
2. Note length

Both of these sequences are terminated with value BB, which restarts the song.

Sample table
=======================================
Samples are used to play sound effects and voice clips on channel 3. They are stored in a table as follows:

1. Priority
2. (Unused)
3-4. Sample start
5-6. Sample end
7. Bank
8. (Unused)

In 720° and Paperboy (GBC), these samples play at about a rate of 3121hz (seemingly prepared at 4000hz). In Marble Madness (GBC) and NFL Blitz games, these play at 8192hz.
